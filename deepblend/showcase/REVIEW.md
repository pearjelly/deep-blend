# Image inspection record

Reviewer: Codex visual inspection of actual local DeepBlend PNGs, 2026-10-09. This is an image-based art-direction record, not a numerical claim of artistic quality or a human review.

## Changes made after inspecting previews

- **Amber Atlas:** the first camera cropped the bottle lip and tray too tightly. The revised camera leaves margin above the lip and separates both bottles. A lower fill and softer key preserve the warm glass edge without turning the entire tray into a white patch.
- **Solstice:** the staged lamp initially had an elevated cable extending beyond the plinth. Its generator path now rests on the plinth, descends outside its edge and returns to the floor. Metal color and roughness were moderated after inspecting the shade reflections.
- **Nocturne:** the first hero used shallow depth of field focused inside the enclosure. The final hero uses f/32 so the woven grille, dial, controls and front edges are sharp together. The separate detail view keeps shallow focus to emphasize the woven geometry.

First, middle and final camera poses were previewed separately. Matching neutral-clay inspections were rendered through the installed plugin; they do not change the scene revisions.

## Film inspection

All three films were played in the local gallery with the browser's native video player. Each reported a six-second duration and 1920×1080 dimensions, with no playback error. The first, middle and final decoded frame hashes differ in each film. Solstice's final encoded frame preserves the full lamp silhouette and the contact between the base and plinth. Nocturne's first, middle and final frames preserve the cabinet silhouette, readable woven threads, dial markings and grounded feet as the camera moves.

The animated WebP previews sample the delivered videos at 12 fps for repository display; the MP4 files retain all 144 rendered frames at 24 fps. A loop preview repeats the six-second camera move and does not imply a seamless camera path.
