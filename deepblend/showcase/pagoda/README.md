# 古塔庭院 / Courtyard Pagoda

A 37.5-second, five-shot pagoda courtyard film rendered from DeepBlend project revision r0075. The shots move from the full courtyard to the eaves, Buddha, water court and a closing garden view. The film contains 900 rendered frames at 24 fps, 1280 × 720, using Cycles with 128 samples. The published MP4 is the verified plugin delivery; its SHA-256, media properties and selected frame sources are recorded in the [showcase manifest](../../docs/assets/showcase/pagoda/manifest.json) and [delivery receipt](../../docs/assets/showcase/pagoda/delivery.json). The receipt preserves the production records with local command paths removed.

The latest rendered video and selected key frames are available on the [case page](https://pearjelly.github.io/deep-blend/pagoda/). The current structured scene input is [`scene-spec.json`](../../docs/assets/showcase/pagoda/scene-spec.json). Attribution and asset scope are in [CREDITS.md](../../docs/assets/showcase/pagoda/CREDITS.md).

The Buddha and source textures are from the Minneapolis Institute of Art CC0 model; supporting asset licenses are recorded in CREDITS.md. An operator-supplied reference image informed the work but is not redistributed. This is a project demonstration and does not imply endorsement by the cited institutions.

The published SceneSpec is structural source only: it records scene layout, materials, cameras and animation, but its external asset files and the native Blender project are not included. It is not a complete replay package.

The film comes from an iterated production project. The published delivery receipt records frame completeness and technical media verification; it does not certify visual quality or a fresh replay of the scene.
