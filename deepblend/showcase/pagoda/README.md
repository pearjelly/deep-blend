# 古塔庭院 / Courtyard Pagoda

A 37.5-second, five-shot pagoda courtyard film rendered from DeepBlend project revision r0075. The shots move from the full courtyard to the eaves, Buddha, water court and a closing garden view. The film contains 900 rendered frames at 24 fps, 1280 × 720, using Cycles with 128 samples. The published MP4 is the verified plugin delivery; its SHA-256 and media properties are recorded in the adjacent showcase manifest.

The latest rendered video and selected key frames are available on the [case page](https://pearjelly.github.io/deep-blend/pagoda/). The current structured scene input is [`scene-spec.json`](../../docs/assets/showcase/pagoda/scene-spec.json). Attribution and asset scope are in [CREDITS.md](../../../docs/assets/showcase/pagoda/CREDITS.md).

The Buddha and source textures are from the Minneapolis Institute of Art CC0 model; supporting asset licenses are recorded in CREDITS.md. An operator-supplied reference image informed the work but is not redistributed. This is a project demonstration and does not imply endorsement by the cited institutions.

The visual review covered all 30 sequential contact sheets for 900 frames and 26 full-size selected frames. The recorded review found no unexpected major subject crops, occlusion or camera intersections in the checked views; this is a bounded review, not a pixel-level inspection of every frame. The film is an existing production handover, not a claim that a one-line prompt created the scene without iteration.
