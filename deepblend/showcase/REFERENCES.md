# Visual references / 视觉参考

Reviewed on 2026-10-09. Reference page: [Blender official Demo Files](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/).

These original product scenes use the local DeepBlend 0.3.2 plugin's procedural geometry, public SceneSpec, material nodes and actual Cycles renders. No official Blender scene, texture, thumbnail or movie frame is included in the delivered artwork.

| Official example | What informed the art direction | Our application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cozy Kitchen — Nicole Morena](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/#splash) | A coherent warm palette, soft window light, tactile ceramic surfaces and layered depth | Amber Atlas: ivory glaze, warm sandstone, champagne metal and transparent glass with carefully placed bright edges |
| [Lone Monk — Carlo Bergonzini / Monorender](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/#cycles) | Architectural repetition, controlled tonal hierarchy and light as the main compositional device | Solstice: repeated backdrop flutes, a dark green field and distinct reflections shaping spun gold metal |
| [Classroom — Christophe Seux](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/#cycles) | Indirect illumination, surfaces with different reflectance, readable detail and contact shadows | Nocturne: dark enamel, genuine interwoven threads, machined metal and a grounded clay podium |

The observations above are design interpretations from the official examples, not statements of endorsement or a claim of matching their production quality. The final images must be judged from their actual pixels. All generated frames, material/geometry changes and video delivery details are recorded alongside the showcase.
