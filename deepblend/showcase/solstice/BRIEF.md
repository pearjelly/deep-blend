# Solstice · 金色暮光

A sculptural champagne-gold desk lamp in a dark botanical-green architectural set. Spun metal, fine circular anisotropy, porcelain enamel, a warm practical light and grounded cable. Golden edge highlights against a restrained green background, premium editorial framing. Six-second true camera arc, preserving the full silhouette.

## Visual acceptance

- Clear silhouette, deliberate framing and adequate margin in the first, middle and final frames.
- Highlight shape describes real curvature; reflective parts remain distinct from the background.
- Contact shadows and material texture remain readable at full resolution.
- No clipping, floating accessories, accidental intersections, or unstable frame-to-frame noise.
- A real 144-frame camera movement at 24 fps: 6 seconds, 1920×1080, Cycles.
- A 2560×1440 hero still and a separate material/detail view.

## Source

Geometry starts from the project's MIT `metal-lamp` recipe and is re-authored through DeepBlend SceneSpec. Styling and staging are original. See [visual references](../REFERENCES.md).
