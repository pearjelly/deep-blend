# Nocturne · 织声

A handcrafted dark evergreen tabletop speaker with true interwoven threads, warm copper anodized bezel, machined knob, enamel cabinet and tiny amber status lens. A terracotta architectural podium and black-green set create warm/cool separation. Precise rim highlights and readable woven detail. Six-second restrained orbit and dolly with a real dial motion.

## Visual acceptance

- Clear silhouette, deliberate framing and adequate margin in the first, middle and final frames.
- Highlight shape describes real curvature; reflective parts remain distinct from the background.
- Contact shadows and material texture remain readable at full resolution.
- No clipping, floating accessories, accidental intersections, or unstable frame-to-frame noise.
- A real 144-frame camera movement at 24 fps: 6 seconds, 1920×1080, Cycles.
- A 2560×1440 hero still and a separate material/detail view.
