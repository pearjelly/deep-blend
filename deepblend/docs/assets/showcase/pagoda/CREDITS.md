# Credits and asset scope

The seated Buddha and its source textures are from the Minneapolis Institute of Art open model, released under CC0 1.0. Source record: [Zenodo 10337923](https://zenodo.org/records/10337923).

Supporting plants, ground, roof, wood, stone and sky materials are CC0 assets from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license). Individual asset records are retained in the production handover.

An operator-supplied pagoda reference image was used only as visual input. It is not included here and no reuse rights are claimed for it. The published stills and film are renders of the authored 3D scene.

New scene structure and case documentation are distributed under this repository's MIT license. Third-party assets retain their respective licenses.
