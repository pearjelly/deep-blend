Conclusion: this version does not use René's blender-cli project. "Blender CLI"
in the generic sense of invoking Blender's executable must not be confused with
the separate blender-cli project. This is a proposed comparison, not an integration.

Evidence at the pinned commit:
- packages/deepblend/provider-local/lib/index.js, startFrameSequence(), lines
  1813–1855: constructs argv containing the Blender executable, --background,
  --factory-startup, --python bootstrap.py, request/plan/result/event/process files,
  then calls ctx.subprocess.spawn().
- packages/deepblend/provider-local/python/bootstrap.py, action_render_frames(),
  lines 365–403: dispatches to deepblend_frames.render_frames().
- packages/deepblend/provider-local/python/deepblend_frames.py, lines 330–336:
  scene.frame_set(frame), then bpy.ops.render.render(write_still=True).
- packages/deepblend/host/lib/render-reconciler.js: identifies/stops orphan,
  rebuilds frame ledger, leaves the job recovering without automatic resume.
- packages/deepblend/host/lib/index.js, resumeRenderJob(): loads record.revisionId,
  computes frames from the disk ledger, and launches that saved job/checkpoint.
- packages/deepblend/host/lib/delivery-manifest.js: source SceneSpec/checkpoint
  SHA-256 values, scene digest, revision ID, frame provenance and probed video facts.
- No blender-cli / blender_cli / blender.cli references found in tracked package
  source or the committed development runtime package-lock.json. Package manifests
  declare DSH/Cordis/contracts and sharp dependencies, with no blender-cli dependency.
